What We Offer

Six Ways We Care

Community Café

Warm drinks, light bites, and a table always ready. No agenda — just rest and connection.

Pastoral Support

Compassionate listening and spiritual care for those walking through grief, illness, or uncertainty.

Practical Aid

Navigation help for hospital visits, community resources, and day-to-day needs during hard seasons.

Prayer & Reflection

A quiet corner for prayer, meditation, or simply sitting in stillness — whatever you need.

Group Gatherings

Regular circles, workshops, and shared meals — because healing is rarely a solo journey.

Family Care

Support for caregivers and families — you don't need to hold everyone alone.
