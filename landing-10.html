Our Heart

Grace Has
No Appointment

We exist near Credit Valley Hospital because some of life's heaviest moments happen nearby. We want to be the warm light at the edge of those moments — a café table, a kind word, a prayer, a hand.

"Every cup of tea poured here is an act of love. Every conversation, an offering."
