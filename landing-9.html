How We Serve

Six Ways of Grace

Community Café

A warm table, quality drinks, and no pressure to leave. Rest here as long as you need.

Pastoral Support

Compassionate listening and spiritual care through grief, illness, and difficult seasons.

Practical Aid

Help navigating hospital systems, community resources, and practical day-to-day needs.

Prayer & Reflection

A quiet corner for prayer, meditation, or simply sitting in still, sacred space.

Group Gatherings

Regular circles, shared meals, and community events. Healing happens together.

Family Care

Support for caregivers and families — you deserve care too, not just the patient.
